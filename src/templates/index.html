Welcome to the Breast Cancer Prediction App

Breast cancer is a complex disease where cells in the breast divide and multiply in an uncontrolled manner, often forming a tumor that can be classified as either benign (non-cancerous) or malignant (cancerous). Early and accurate diagnosis is critical, as it significantly increases the chances of successful treatment and long-term survival.

To assist us in this diagnostic process, our project utilizes the Wisconsin Breast Cancer Diagnostic (WBCD) dataset, a cornerstone of medical machine learning. This dataset consists of 569 samples with 30 numerical features derived from digitized images of fine needle aspirates (FNA) of breast masses. These features describe the geometric properties of cell nuclei—such as radius, texture, perimeter, area, and smoothness—measuring the mean, standard error, and "worst" values for each characteristic. Because these physical dimensions differ significantly between healthy and cancerous cells, AI models can effectively learn to distinguish the two classes, providing a powerful tool for automated medical screening.

This application allows you to predict whether a breast tumor is malignant or benign based on various features. Below you will have to fill in the feature values for a new sample and then submit the form. A trained model will verify the entries and will predict the outcome.
